North Sea Jazz —
In Good Company

North Sea Jazz Festival · Communication Strategy · 2024

Challenge

North Sea Jazz sits in the top 10 of the Netherlands' strongest events, with programming and heritage nobody can replicate. But among young urban music lovers, brand preference and visitor intent were low — a real problem for the festival's future. The research told a counterintuitive story: young people actually love jazz and what it represents. When you walked them through what NSJ already offers, they found it compelling. The problem wasn't the festival. It was that NSJ wasn't showing itself in a way that reached this audience — and a misperception had quietly taken hold. They assumed it wasn't for them, because nothing had ever suggested otherwise.

Approach

The strategy positioned North Sea Jazz as the passionate thought leader of musical and creative craftsmanship — an authority with something to say year-round, not just when tickets go on sale. Various content initiatives closed the gap between perception and reality: The Heartbeat of NSJ, a content series giving voice to the artists and makers that make the festival what it is; a poster campaign putting portraits of the real NSJ crowd — diverse, dynamic, unmistakably cool — in the spaces where young audiences actually spend their time; and social video content bringing the festival energy into the feeds of a new generation. The message across all of it was simple: people like you are already here.

Outcome

A communication platform that gave NSJ the tools to actively counter the misperception holding back a new generation of visitors, and sustain that effort over time. By leading with culture rather than commerce, the strategy built a credible bridge between an extraordinary legacy and an audience already primed to love it.
