Philips OneBlade —
One of a Kind

Philips OneBlade · Campaign Strategy · 2023

Challenge

OneBlade launched in 2016 by picking a fight with the entire shaving category. It worked. Five years on, the category it created was full of imitators. Which is flattering, but not a strategy. The task was to shift from challenging to leading. The complication: men are not wandering around looking for a new way to shave. They have a method, however ancient and however objectively grim, and they are sticking with it.

Approach

The answer was on Amazon. OneBlade was the number one best-rated shaver online, with over 50,000 reviews from real customers — some so enthusiastic they were almost hard to believe. Almost. The product might not have been as differentiated as it once was, but the stories people told about it were genuinely one of a kind. So real reviews became the campaign. A series of short films, dramatised and slightly absurd, based entirely on things actual users wrote. A locker room full of dancing men. A man in full Shakespearean crisis over whether to shave. Stranger than fiction, because they were true.

Outcome

Turns out the best way to get men interested in a shaver is to let other men do the talking. Brand recognition hit 36% above target in the UK, with similar results across all markets. Ad recall doubled. And in a piece of logic the campaign probably deserved, people started leaving reviews about the review-based campaign. The work won a Bronze Lamp at the Dutch Creativity Awards, in both Craft and Strategy.
